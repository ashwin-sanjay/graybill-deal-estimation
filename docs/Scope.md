# Mathematical scope

This release presents 34 result groups and twenty named final results for the Graybill–Deal common-mean problem and its mathematical dependencies. The [statement-to-evidence guide](Evidence.md) gives the exact hypotheses and competitor classes for each result.

The public sources distinguish Lean statements under their actual premises, complete written arguments, and finite or directed numerical certificates. Mathematical support modules can serve several statements; their inclusion does not add statistical claims beyond the displayed statements.

The [written proof guide](Proofs.md) lists the five analytic arguments and supporting mathematical notes. Original attribution and the [license scopes](../LICENSE) remain applicable.
